Privacy Policy — FutHub

Last updated: 30 August 2026

FutHub is an independent Android app that shows football scores, statistics, fixtures and broadcast information. The data controller is Pedro Marques, FutHub’s independent developer, who can be contacted at pmiguelrmarquesdev@gmail.com. This policy explains the data processed when you use the app.

Requesting data deletion

To request deletion of your data, email pmiguelrmarquesdev@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion — FutHub”. If you contacted us before, use the same address so that we can locate the correspondence. Do not send device identifiers or additional information that is not necessary.

  • Data deleted by the developer: we will delete contact messages and attachments that we can associate with the requesting address. They are normally retained for up to 12 months after the matter is closed, but may be erased sooner following a valid request, unless a legal obligation or the defence of legal claims requires retention.
  • Local data: your club, favourites, preferences and cache exist only on the device and cannot be deleted remotely by us. Delete them in Android settings by clearing FutHub’s data, or uninstall the app. An existing Android backup controlled by Google may later restore them.
  • Provider technical data: pseudonymous installation or advertising identifiers, notification tokens, IP address, usage and diagnostic data processed by Google or API-Sports are not linked by us to your email. If we cannot relate them to you, we cannot locate or erase them based only on the request. You can change consent under Settings → Privacy options, reset or delete the Advertising ID in Android settings, and exercise your rights directly with the relevant provider.
  • Data that does not exist: FutHub has no user accounts and does not store names, email addresses, personal profiles, favourites or preferences linked to a person in Firestore.

We will reply with the outcome of our checks, stating what was deleted and, where applicable, which data we could not identify or had to retain and why.

1. Data you save in the app

FutHub requires no account and does not send us your name, email address or a list of your choices. The app stores locally on the device:

  • your selected club, including its identifier, name and crest address;
  • favourite teams and notification preferences;
  • language, tutorial completion and update-notice preferences;
  • cached images and other technical data used to improve performance.

Depending on device settings, Android may include app data in a Google Account backup or a device-to-device transfer and restore it after reinstallation. Android/Google, not FutHub, controls this process. You can manage backups in the device settings and delete local data through Android settings or by uninstalling the app; an existing backup may later be restored.

2. Services needed to operate the app

  • Firebase Cloud Firestore: provides matches, standings, profiles and minimum-version configuration. FutHub does not include your personal choices in these reads. Google may receive the IP address, app identifiers and technical connection data.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: when you enable notifications, the installation subscribes to match topics. Google processes an installation identifier/registration token and technical data to deliver kick-off, goal and full-time alerts. We do not store the token in our own database or link it to your identity.
  • API-Football/API-Sports: sports data reaches the app through our backend, but crests and photographs may be downloaded directly from API-Sports image servers. Those servers necessarily receive technical data such as the IP address and image request.
  • Google Play: the app may check for and present available updates; Google Play manages installation.

3. Usage measurement (Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate app use, diagnose trends and improve features. The SDK automatically records events such as first launch, sessions, app opens and updates, together with device model, operating system, language, approximate geographic location and a random app-instance identifier. On Android, it may also collect the Advertising ID when available. We do not set a user name or try to identify you directly from this data.

4. Advertising and privacy choices (Google AdMob/UMP)

FutHub uses Google AdMob to display ads. The ads SDK may process the IP address (including to estimate general location), the Advertising ID and other device/account identifiers, interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostic data, to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud and abuse.

Where required in your region, Google’s User Messaging Platform presents a form before ads are requested and records your choices. Depending on your choice and applicable law, ads may be personalised, non-personalised or limited. When Google requires a reopening entry point, you can change or withdraw your choice under Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete the Advertising ID in Android settings.

5. When you contact us

The Contact screen prepares a message and hands it to the device’s email app; FutHub neither sends nor stores it before you confirm sending. If you send it, we receive the sender address, content and any data you choose to include. We use it only to reply, provide support and investigate or fix the reported issue. Do not send confidential information or another person’s personal data.

6. Purposes and legal bases

We process technical data needed to provide requested features, such as loading content, delivering notifications and checking for updates. We process contact messages to answer your request. We use aggregate measurement, reliability and fraud prevention based on our legitimate interests in operating, protecting and improving FutHub, where those interests do not override your rights. For personalised advertising and other processing where required by law, the basis is consent collected through the privacy form. You may withdraw consent without affecting previous lawful processing.

7. Recipients and international transfers

Data may be processed by Google (Firebase, Analytics, AdMob, UMP, Google Play and Android backups) and API-Sports (image hosting), as needed for the services above. These providers may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area, using the transfer mechanisms and safeguards described in their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase privacy and security
  • AdMob privacy
  • API-Sports Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal data.

8. Retention

Local data remains until you change a choice, clear app data or uninstall, subject to possible Android backup and restoration. Notification subscriptions remain until you disable them or the identifier expires or is deleted. Google and API-Sports retain technical data under their policies and retention settings. We keep contact messages only as long as needed to reply and handle the matter, normally up to 12 months after closure, unless the law or the defence of legal claims requires longer.

9. Your rights

Where the GDPR or equivalent law applies, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and withdraw consent. Because we have no accounts or database linking choices to a person, we may need details from your message to locate earlier correspondence and may be unable to link you to pseudonymous data held solely by a provider. Exercise your rights by email below. You may also complain to the Portuguese Data Protection Authority (CNPD) or your local supervisory authority.

10. Children

FutHub is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data submitted directly by children. If you believe a child sent us personal data, contact us so that we can delete it.

11. Security

We use services with encrypted transmission and limit processing to necessary data. No system is entirely secure; we cannot guarantee the absolute security of transmissions, devices or third-party services.

12. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app, providers or law. We will publish the current version and date here. Where a change is material, we will seek to highlight it in the app or by another appropriate means.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact pmiguelrmarquesdev@gmail.com.
